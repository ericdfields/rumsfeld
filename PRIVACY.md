# Privacy policy

The `rumsfeld` plugin does not collect, store, or send any data.

- It contains only instructions and Markdown templates for Claude.
- It has no code, no MCP servers, no hooks, and no network calls of its own.
- It writes its report (`RUMSFELD.md`) only to your own project.

Claude processes your documents under the terms of the Claude product you use. See [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/ericdfields/rumsfeld/issues).
